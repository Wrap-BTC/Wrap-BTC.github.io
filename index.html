What is Wrap BTC?

WBTC is Wrapped Bitcoin, an ERC-20 token that represents Bitcoin on Ethereum; its public token page is available on Etherscan. Use the official WBTC Network destination to confirm current mint, redemption, merchant, and custody information before moving funds.

Wrap BTC to WBTC on Ethereum

Wrapping BTC usually means converting native Bitcoin into a tokenized representation such as Wrapped Bitcoin (WBTC) so it can be used on Ethereum and other smart-contract networks. WBTC is designed to track BTC 1:1, but the process is not a native Bitcoin transaction: BTC is held through the WBTC merchant and custodian system, and WBTC is minted as an ERC-20 token. Most retail users get WBTC through a centralized exchange, OTC desk, DeFi route, or bridge rather than minting directly. The key checks are token contract, network, spread, custody model, and whether the route delivers canonical WBTC or a bridged version of it.

Wrap BTC fees and spread

Wrap BTC costs come from several layers: Bitcoin network fees, exchange or merchant spread, Ethereum gas, and any bridge or swap fee if the route crosses chains. WBTC itself is intended to represent BTC at a 1:1 reserve level, but users can still pay a premium or discount when acquiring it on exchanges or DEX pools. Large swaps should compare order-book liquidity, DEX slippage, and withdrawal fees before execution. For small transfers, gas can dominate the cost, especially on Ethereum mainnet. A good route shows the received WBTC amount, network, contract address, and all fees before the user signs.

Wrap BTC supported networks

WBTC started as an Ethereum ERC-20 asset and is now visible across many ecosystems through canonical deployments, bridged tokens, or exchange-supported withdrawals. CoinGecko lists WBTC contract data across Ethereum, Base, BNB Chain, Avalanche, Optimism, Solana, Sui, Tron, and other networks, but not every version has the same custody or bridge assumptions. Users should treat each chain as a separate asset address, even when the ticker says WBTC. Sending WBTC on the wrong network can make funds difficult or impossible to recover, so confirm the chain in the wallet, exchange deposit screen, and block explorer before transferring.

Wrap BTC FAQ

Can I wrap BTC directly from a Bitcoin wallet?

Most users do not mint WBTC directly from a Bitcoin wallet. They usually acquire WBTC through an exchange, DeFi swap, OTC desk, or a bridge route that handles the conversion flow. Direct WBTC minting is generally part of the merchant and custodian process, so retail users should focus on verified token addresses and reputable routes.

Is WBTC backed by real Bitcoin?

WBTC is designed as a Bitcoin-backed token where minted WBTC corresponds to BTC held in custody. Users should still verify current reserve dashboards, token supply, and custodian disclosures because the assurance model is operational rather than native to Bitcoin. That makes WBTC useful, but not identical to holding BTC on the Bitcoin chain.

What happens when I unwrap WBTC?

Unwrapping WBTC means moving from the tokenized WBTC position back toward native BTC through a route that burns or redeems WBTC and releases BTC. The exact flow depends on the exchange, merchant, bridge, or custodian route being used. Users should confirm redemption fees, minimums, KYC requirements, and settlement time before starting.

Can I send WBTC to a BTC address?

No. WBTC on Ethereum or another smart-contract network should not be sent directly to a native Bitcoin address. It must be sent to an address on the same network where the token exists, or redeemed through a service that explicitly converts it to BTC. A network mismatch can lead to permanent loss.

Why use WBTC instead of selling BTC for ETH?

WBTC lets users keep Bitcoin-denominated exposure while accessing Ethereum DeFi. Instead of selling BTC for ETH, a user can hold a BTC-tracking ERC-20 token and supply it to lending markets, DEX pools, or collateral systems. That benefit comes with additional smart-contract, liquidity, and custodian risk compared with native BTC.

What is the WBTC Ethereum contract address?

The commonly referenced WBTC contract on Ethereum is 0x2260FAC5E5542a773Aa44fBCfeDf7C193bc2C599. Users should still verify it through WBTC, Etherscan, CoinGecko, or another trusted source before importing a token. Contract addresses are chain-specific; a valid Ethereum address does not verify a WBTC token on another network.
